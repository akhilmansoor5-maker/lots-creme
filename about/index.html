About

A pâtisserie in Manjeri

French pastry, coffee, waffles, and cakes — made by hand.

The studio

A cafe for pastry, and a bench for cakes

You can see the shop from the road: a lit sign, plants along the windows, a small terrace to the side. Inside, the work is pastry, dessert, and cakes.

Come in for coffee or something from the case. For a birthday or a wedding, send the date on WhatsApp.

The terrace

A place beside the cafe

The terrace is part of the shop: tables outside, the party hall at the same address. Coffee and pastry are served in the cafe. Cakes for a date are made on the bench.

Open daily, 10 am to 11 pm

The team

The team behind Lots. Crème

The shop is run from the bench at Kovilamkundu. Pastry, coffee, waffles, and cakes are made by hand, in the same room.

For a cake on a date, or the party hall, write on WhatsApp.
